- Decided by the PG1 builder, 2026-11-09, lightly held: folded vs gone is a share of the gone sentence's content
  words (lowercased, `stemTerm`, a small function-word list in `self/set-down.ts`). Reworded, kept: ≥ 0.7 of them in
  one new sentence or two side by side, whatever the writer named (a line named with another why but found
  reworded is kept, not recorded; one named "folded" is counted folded). Folded, kept: the writer said "folded …"
  and ≥ 0.5 sit in a run of up to three new sentences. Otherwise set down, named or "set down, unnamed"; a named
  fold that isn't found is recorded with "said folded, but not found". Why: the brief's "no silent drop, whatever
  the writer says", and a word share is the cheapest judgement a reviewer can re-run (`tools/set-down-diff.ts`). The
  numbers are unmeasured defaults (`SET_DOWN_LIMITS`); CHECK-AFTER carries their first calibration.
