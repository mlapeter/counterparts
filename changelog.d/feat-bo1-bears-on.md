- **A lasting fact about a person can say what else it bears on.** `remember` and each
  `session_end` entry take an optional `bearsOn`, which lists the everyday areas a fact changes advice
  about without naming them (vegan: cooking, restaurants, gifts). They are kept as the
  memory's last paragraph, `Also about: …`, so a question about cooking can find a vegan fact
  that was written about leather. They are kept only on a memory about you, the two of you, or
  a person, and an area the text already names is dropped. A revision keeps them unless it
  sends its own.
