- **An open item is no longer put in front of you every day.** "Still open" at the start of a session now lists an
  open item the day it is new, then again only after 3 and 7 more days, and then parks it: counted as parked, not
  listed. It is the same schedule a question waiting on you already followed when a conversation touched it, and one
  record for both, so an item already raised that way counts. Nothing is closed or deleted: `recall "still open"` still
  lists every item, the parked ones marked. Items with a date keep following their date.
