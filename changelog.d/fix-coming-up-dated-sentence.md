- **"Coming up" names a dated memory by the sentence that holds its date.** A memory with no title (or one titled by
  the start of its first sentence) whose first sentence is about something else is listed by the later sentence that
  names its day, such as "Marisol is coming home Friday 16 to Sunday 18 October", shortened to about a title's length,
  in the wake and in recall "coming up". A memory with a real title still shows its title, and a repeating reminder
  keeps its first line. A number on its own ("3rd floor", "16GB", "PR #16") doesn't count as a date.
