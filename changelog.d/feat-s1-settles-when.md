- **An open item can say what would settle it.** Beside `unresolved: true`, `remember` and
  `session_end` entries take an optional `settlesWhen`, a short phrase like "Katie says how the
  reading went". "Still open", in the wake and in `recall "still open"`, shows it after the
  item ("… · settles when Katie says how the reading went"). The nightly reflection is shown
  open items that name one, even ones touched recently, and closes an item when a memory
  plainly shows that thing happened, with a why and the way back. Items without one behave
  exactly as before. `status` counts how many open items name one and how many don't.
