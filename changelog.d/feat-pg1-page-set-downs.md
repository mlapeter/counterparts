- **The page says what it sets down.** The self page stays within its room, so each night a new line pushes an old
  one out. Now the night's page writers name the lines they take out, with a short why, and anything taken out
  that wasn't named is recorded anyway; a line only reworded, or folded into a story still on the page, isn't
  counted as gone. Each line set down is kept as a memory, word for word. The next morning's wake names up to two
  ("Set down from my page last night: …"), and `recall "set down"` lists them all, newest first, with why and date.
  When life brings one up again (it's used, or a newer memory says it again), the night's writers are shown it and
  may put it back.
