- **The page's stories are about growth, not only corrections.** On a store that keeps its page in its own form
  (`counterparts page-form own`), the night's page writers are told to keep a mix of story kinds (corrections that
  changed how the counterpart works, and also what went right, discoveries, and moments with the owner), and that a
  story earns its place if it would still teach something in a different project. Day's end is shown the day's
  memories about me or us with a small head start over the day's work, so a busy work day doesn't crowd them out.
  A practice on the page that is only about the work may move into a skill memory, which recall brings back when
  the work calls for it; the set-down record names it. On every store, `remember` takes `story: true` to nominate a
  moment as a story: the night's writer sees it first and still decides, and one it passes by fades like any
  other memory.
