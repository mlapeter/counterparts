- **"Today, elsewhere" and "Since you started" say what the other folders are working on.**
  Beside each folder's memory count they name up to two of its memories by title and id,
  work in progress first ("planned" or "done" marks, then work memories), newest first:
  `~/random open since 08:33 (live), 8 memories: "Wake check 10-20" (mem_…), "Spans out of
  backups" (mem_…)`. Never from a folder that is off, paused or observed, never a
  confidential memory, one about you, about the two of you or about your counterpart
  itself, or small talk. "Since you started" names
  only what was written since it last told you. Both lines stay within their old size.
