- **`counterparts mechanisms` says how often a settled thing comes back.** A new line under the
  mechanisms: of the memories changed or corrected before this week, how many a session drew on
  the old side of again, how many came up again (a new disagreement, or a new memory saying the
  old thing), and how many were only shown, split by the build that wrote them. `--json` prints
  the short view as JSON, with example ids.
