- **The wake knows yesterday's news.** With the wake's shape on, a session start now opens, after the page, with
  "Since the last night:": up to three of the strongest things about you, or about the two of you, saved since the last
  night ran, from any folder that's on. Something said in another folder this morning (a launch, good news from the vet)
  no longer waits a night to reach the next session. Nothing new, no section.
- **An old handoff stops leading the wake.** Half a day after a folder's last handoff, once another session has worked
  there, the handoff shows as one line with its age and how many sessions came since, after what they did, instead of
  whole and first for days.
- **No empty sections in the wake.** "Nothing open.", "Nothing dated." and "Open here now: no other session." are gone,
  with their headings: a section with nothing in it isn't printed.
- **Something that already happened stops being "due today".** When a newer memory marked done says a dated thing
  happened (the shop launched a day early), its reminder isn't told and it leaves Coming up.
