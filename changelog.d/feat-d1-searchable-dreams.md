- **Dreams can be remembered by what they were about.** Asking about something a recent dream
  was about ("keys", "locked doors") brings that dream back beside the answer, even when the
  question never says "dream": one at most, and only when its meaning is close. It is
  labelled `[dream]` with its date and id, and said to be dreamed, not lived, so it is told as
  a dream and never as something that happened. A question about dreaming finds dreams by
  their content too, and "when did you last dream?" always names the newest one, including
  one from earlier the same day.
- **Dreams fade unless they are talked about.** A dream nobody recalls drops out of reach by
  what it was about after a day or so; one that is read, asked about or told stays longer
  each day it is. It is never deleted: "last dream" and its id still read it.
- **A dream from a folder that is set off stays there.** Dreams follow the folder setting:
  one written in a folder set off or paused is not shown in another folder's answers.
